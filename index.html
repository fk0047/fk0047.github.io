About me

Between the client, the product and the code.

For nine years I've been the person who makes sure software actually ships. I sit between clients, stakeholders and the dev team, turn business goals into requirements and roadmaps, and keep scope, timelines and quality on track through launch and after it.

I started out writing code myself (web, Android and iOS apps, PHP, Node.js, SolidJS, MySQL, Postgres, Redis and Elasticsearch), so I can follow architecture decisions, review PRs and talk to engineers in their own terms. I've run teams of up to 12 developers, 4 QA and 2 designers across as many as ten projects at once.

Most of my recent work is AI: LLM agents, AI content and campaign generation, AI medical record review, AI pricing tools and automations that remove manual work, built on Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen and Kimi, including open-weight models running locally. I also have a deep Web3 and fintech background in crypto exchanges, NFT marketplaces, wallets and payments. I've been fully remote since 2021.
