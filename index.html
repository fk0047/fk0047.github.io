About

Between the client and the code.

I've spent the last nine years making sure software actually gets delivered. I sit between clients, stakeholders and the dev team, turn business goals into roadmaps, and keep scope, timelines and quality in check until launch.

I started out writing code myself (web, Android and iOS apps, PHP, Node.js, MySQL and Postgres), so I can follow architecture decisions and review PRs.
